# Shanel Crafts PH — Landing Page: Goals

## What this is
A single-page marketing/landing site for **Shanel Crafts PH**, a home-based Cebu (Balamban / Mandaue-adjacent) crafter selling **handmade fuzzy-wire ("chenille stem") flower bouquets** — no real flowers — plus **money garlands** and **money bouquets** (cash folded/rolled into bouquet form, popular as graduation and birthday gifts in the Philippines). Product line also includes small items like flower-frame mirrors and keychains, sold both online (Facebook page, GCash) and at pop-up market stalls.

This is **not** an e-commerce checkout. It's a landing page whose job is to turn a visitor into a **Facebook Messenger inquiry / GCash order lead**.

## Who it's for
- People searching for a **unique, lasting, "never wilts" gift** for graduation, birthday, anniversary, Valentine's — most likely browsing on **mobile**, often arriving from a Facebook or Instagram post/ad.
- Parents/relatives of **graduates** (money garlands, lei garlands) during grad season — a clear seasonal spike.
- Repeat local customers who already know the brand from market stalls and want to see the **catalog + how to order** in one place.

## Primary goal
Get the visitor to **message the shop on Facebook Messenger or DM on Instagram** with a specific product or custom request in mind — as fast and frictionlessly as possible. These are the shop's only two order channels; the page should never send someone anywhere else to "buy."

## Secondary goals
1. **Showcase, not sell.** This is a portfolio/showcase site first — the goal is to make the range and craftsmanship of the work (fuzzy-wire bouquets especially, plus money bouquets, money garlands, lei garlands, mirror keepsakes) obvious and trustworthy, not to run a storefront. No prices, no cart.
2. Communicate that **flowers don't wilt** — the core differentiator vs. real-flower florists, and the reason fuzzy-wire is the signature line.
3. Make it dead simple to understand **how customization works** (colors, wording, "Class of 2026" ribbons, occasion) and **how to order** (message on FB/IG → describe the occasion & colors → get a quote → pay via GCash → pickup/deliver).
4. Build light social proof (the market-stall photo, "Est. 2024", the FB/IG presence).
5. Stay **evergreen year-round** — not led by grad season, even though several assets are grad-themed. Grad items are one part of a year-round catalog, not the seasonal hook.

## Non-goals (explicitly out of scope for this landing page)
- No cart, no online payment processing, no accounts/login.
- No blog/CMS, no multi-page site nav (single scrolling page with anchor links only).
- No real-time inventory or pricing engine — pricing shown is illustrative/"starting at," final price confirmed in chat.

## Success looks like
- A visitor can go from landing → understanding the product range → tapping "Message us" (FB or IG) in **under 30 seconds**, with zero confusion about what's being sold (fuzzy-wire ≠ real flowers) or how to order.
- Page loads fast and looks intentional/handcrafted on a phone screen first (most traffic is mobile, from FB/IG).

## Language
Copy is written in clear, warm **English**. See `design.md` for tone notes.

## Content the page needs (source material)
- Hero: `s1.jpg` (money-garland bouquet — the site's signature "wow" shot).
- Product galleries: fuzzy-wire flower bouquets (colorways: pastel mixed, blue/white, red/black "elegant," sunflower, tulip mix), money bouquets/garlands, lei/graduation garlands, mirror keepsakes.
- Labeled "anatomy" shots (`s14`–`s16`) showing named flower types (gerbera, hibiscus, mini daisy, carnation, gypsophilia, sunflower, etc.) — great raw material for a "what we make" or "flower guide" section.
- Logo (`s-logo.jpg`) for palette + brand mark.
- Real-world market stall photo (`s18`) for a "find us / trust" section.

## Decisions locked in
- **Signature product:** fuzzy-wire flower bouquets (hero section, largest showcase). Money bouquets, money garlands, and lei garlands are secondary catalog sections.
- **Order channels:** Facebook Messenger and Instagram DM only — no other platforms, no on-site checkout.
- **No pricing shown anywhere** on the page.
- **Evergreen**, not grad-season-led.
- **Copy language:** English.
